# Khandoli page: sources and what is (not) claimed

## Facts used, and where they come from

| Statement on the site | Source |
|---|---|
| Khandoli is a dam, reservoir and hill area in Giridih district, Jharkhand; the reservoir is known as Khandoli Lake | Wikipedia "Khandoli Dam"; Giridih district site giridih.nic.in/tourist-place/khandoli |

| Many guides suggest roughly October to March | TravelSetu / Sygic guides |

## Deliberately not stated

- **Distances and travel times** (sources disagree: roughly 8-10 km from Giridih town is quoted). Ask the resort for directions.
- **Activities such as parasailing, paragliding, kayaking or rock climbing**: listed inconsistently by sources, not verified locally.
- **Specific bird species or counts**, and any guarantee of sightings. The photo of geese is captioned "not guaranteed"; the species is not named.
- **The name of the hill** in the photographs, or that every hill photo shows the same hill.
- **Views from the resort**: only that one room's balcony photograph shows a hill and lake; "not guaranteed for every room".
- Boating hours, fees, opening times, entry fees, safety and access.
